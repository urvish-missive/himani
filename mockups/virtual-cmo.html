    Do you execute, or only advise?

    We already have an agency. Does that work?

    Yes. I give your agencies one plan and one scorecard, so their work adds up instead of running in parallel.

    It starts with a 30-day diagnosis. After that, the leadership engagement runs month to month with a short notice period.

    B2B, SaaS and tech companies that have product-market fit and some marketing in place, but no senior marketing leader. It's less suited to very early teams still testing the idea.

    I help you define the role, interview candidates and hand over the plan, so your new CMO starts with momentum.
