    How every training works

    1. Brief

      Call with the team lead

      We agree on goals, gaps and who attends.

    2. Pre-work

      Before the session

      I review your website, content and materials to build real examples.

    Questions teams ask

    How many people can attend?

    Workshops work best with up to [20] people so everyone gets hands-on time. Larger groups can be split into cohorts.

    Online or on-site?

    Both. Online sessions run on your usual video tool. For on-site workshops I travel to your office; travel costs are agreed upfront.

    How customised is it?

    Every module is rebuilt around your company. Exercises use your website, content, decks and pipeline, not generic examples.

    Can marketing and sales attend together?

    Yes, and it's often the most useful format. Shared sessions fix the messaging gap between the two teams.

    What happens after the workshop?

    Your team gets a playbook, and I hold a follow-up call to review what they've put into practice. The recurring programme adds monthly reviews of real work.
