    Questions founders ask

    Is this coaching or consulting?

    Coaching. You stay in charge of marketing and I help you make better decisions. If you want someone to lead marketing for you, the Virtual CMO engagement fits better.

    Is what we discuss confidential?

    Yes. Your numbers, plans and challenges stay between us. [Add whether you sign an NDA on request.]

    How much time does it take?

    A session every two weeks plus a little preparation. Most founders spend two to three hours a month.

    Do you work on my LinkedIn content directly?

    I review your posts and help you find your voice and themes. Writing them stays with you or your team, so the voice remains yours.

    What if I'm not a B2B or SaaS founder?

    The work applies to most founder-led companies. Apply and tell me about yours; I'll be honest about whether I'm the right coach.
