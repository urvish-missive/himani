Before you hire me

What does a Virtual CMO actually do?

I lead your marketing part-time: I set the strategy and budget, direct your team and agencies, shape brand and messaging, and report results to leadership each month.

How is coaching different from the Virtual CMO role?

As Virtual CMO, I lead your company's marketing. In coaching, you lead it and I help you make better decisions as a founder.

Are trainings one-off or ongoing?

Both. A one-off workshop closes a specific gap. A recurring programme builds habits by reviewing your team's real work every month.

Do you only work on SEO?

No. Content is where I built my name, but I work across marketing strategy, brand, founder-led LinkedIn, sales enablement, AI in marketing, and search.

Do you work with companies outside India?

Yes. Engagements run online across time zones, and I travel for on-site trainings and events.

Is this you or your agency?

Everything on this page is delivered by me personally. If you need a team to execute, Missive Digital can support separately.
